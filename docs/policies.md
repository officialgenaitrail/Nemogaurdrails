# CedarCart demo support policies

These are FICTIONAL policies for a tutorial, not statements about a real company.

## Refunds
Customers can request a refund within 14 calendar days after delivery. Items must be unused and have proof of purchase. Refunds return to the original payment method after approval. Returns requested after 14 days are not eligible for an automatic refund under this demo policy.

## Shipping
Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. International shipping is not currently offered.

## Billing
Receipts and invoices are sent to the email address used at checkout. A customer may contact support to request a duplicate invoice. Support staff must verify account ownership before discussing individual orders.

## Account security
Support agents never request passwords, one-time passwords, API keys, or complete payment-card numbers. Customers must not share secret credentials in the support chat.

## Customer support
Customer support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, at support@example.test. The example.test domain is a placeholder and not a working inbox.
